I am a researcher in the Department of Statistics at Columbia University, where I completed my M.A. in Statistics. Before that, I received a BSc in Financial Mathematics with First-class Honours from Xi'an Jiaotong-Liverpool University.

My research develops statistical methods for data whose observations are probability distributions or high-dimensional time series, using tools from optimal transport. I work with Yisha Yao on these topics.

I am applying to PhD programs in statistics for Fall 2027.

Research interests
======

* Optimal transport and statistics in Wasserstein space
* Distributional data analysis: clustering, anomaly detection, statistical depth
* Multivariate quantiles and distributional forecasting for high-dimensional time series
* Robust statistics and learning
